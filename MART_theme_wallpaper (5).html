Loyalty Program Rules

Default rule: spend ₹100 to earn 1 point; 1 point redeems ₹1. Points are earned on the final amount paid after product discounts and redeemed points.

Every sale is included in one combined Walk-in Customer row, including named customers, phone-only customers, loyalty-card customers and anonymous walk-ins. No individual customer or loyalty-card customer appears as a separate heat-map row. Loyalty card records, balances and purchase history remain saved in the Loyalty Customers section; only this chart combines their sales. Each cell shows the number of bills or net sales amount for its time slot. Daily view shows 24 hourly columns (00:00–23:00). Weekly view shows Monday–Sunday as rows and 00:00–23:00 as columns. Heat map uses the sales records currently saved in this browser.

Product Per-Unit Cost Line Chart

Select a product to see its historical average cost per unit by day in the selected month or year.
Average Unit Cost (₹ / unit)Each point shows the weighted average saved unit cost for that day.
Search for a product by name or barcode, then select it to view the line chart.

The line chart shows only per-unit cost (₹ per unit), not total cost or quantity. Each point is a quantity-weighted average of the saved unit costs for that product on one calendar day in the selected month or year. Historical sale lines without a saved cost are excluded and noted below the chart.
